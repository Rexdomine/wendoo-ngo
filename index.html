About Wendoo

Established in Nigeria.
Building on experience.

Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative was founded by Mrs Maureen Bond, a UK-based public relations professional. She established Wendoo in response to underfed schoolchildren and the barriers hunger can create for learning.

Our history

Wendoo was established as a registered Nigerian NGO in 2022. The School Breakfast Programme launched in February 2023. Its launch and work were documented and reported across the media.

Wendoo is not starting from an idea. It is building on experience and seeking sustainable funding and strategic partnerships to strengthen, restore and expand its activities.

Registration

Registered with Nigeria’s Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) on 31 October 2022.

Registration number: 191090

Leadership and governance

Wendoo is led by its Chairman, who coordinates projects with the oversight and approval of the Trustees. The Secretary supports administration, communications and engagement. A Fundraising Committee is convened with Trustee approval.

Where we work

Wendoo’s objective is to address hunger among schoolchildren in rural and urban areas of Nigeria. Nkeze Primary School in Idumuje Ugboko, Delta State, is the first school for programme development and delivery.

The programme was chosen in response to challenges including lack of running water, derelict buildings and overgrown areas around the school grounds.
