The Morning That Changes Everything

No child should have to learn on an empty stomach.

Wendoo is working to remove hunger as a barrier to learning by helping children start their school day nourished, ready and able to learn.

Support a Child Partner With Wendoo

Hunger doesn’t stop at the school gate.

A child can arrive at school ready to learn — but hunger can make learning harder.

Wendoo exists to help change that.

Our response is simple.
Our ambition is bigger.

Wendoo’s School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative brings together people, partnerships and resources to support children with nutritious breakfasts at the start of the school day.

NOURISH

Provide nutritious meals at the start of the school day so children can arrive nourished and ready.

ENABLE

Create the conditions for learning, participation and confidence to grow from the first moment.

EMPOWER

Work with schools, communities and partners to build sustainable, locally led breakfast support.

SCALE

Extend reach, deepen measurement, and build trust through transparent, story-led reporting.

A nutritious breakfast can be the first step towards a better school day.

BREAKFAST→ ENERGY→ CONCENTRATION→ PARTICIPATION→ LEARNING→ OPPORTUNITY

And a better school day can be the beginning of a brighter future.
