Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative

Hunger doesn’t stop at the school gate.

A child can arrive at school ready to learn — but hunger can make learning harder.

For children living in vulnerable circumstances, a nutritious breakfast is not always guaranteed. Going to school hungry can affect concentration, participation, energy and wellbeing.

And when hunger becomes a barrier to learning, it can become a barrier to opportunity.

Wendoo exists to help change that.

We believe every child deserves to begin the school day with the nourishment and support they need to learn, grow and thrive.

Our response is simple.
Our ambition is bigger.

Through the Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative, we work to expand access to nutritious breakfast support for children who need it most.

But our ambition goes beyond providing a meal.

We are building partnerships that can help create sustainable, scalable solutions to child hunger and educational disadvantage.

Four pillars

Nourish

Helping provide nutritious breakfast support to children.

Enable

Helping children start the school day with energy and readiness to learn.

Empower

Supporting children’s wellbeing, confidence and educational opportunity.

Scale

Bringing communities, organisations and funders together to reach more children.

The Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative

Our programme is designed to help children who may otherwise begin their school day without a nutritious meal.

We work towards building partnerships with schools, communities, businesses, foundations, philanthropists and other organisations to support breakfast provision and develop sustainable models for expansion.

Our approach

  1. Identify — Work with communities and relevant partners to identify need.
  2. Mobilise — Bring together funding, resources, expertise and community support.
  3. Deliver — Support nutritious breakfast provision for participating children.
  4. Measure — Track reach, resources and outcomes.
  5. Grow — Use learning and partnerships to expand the programme sustainably.
