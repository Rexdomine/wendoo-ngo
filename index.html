Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative

Hunger doesn’t stop at the school gate.

Wendoo exists to help change that.

A child can arrive at school ready to learn — but hunger can make learning harder.

For children in vulnerable circumstances, a nutritious breakfast is not always guaranteed. Going to school hungry can affect concentration, energy, participation and wellbeing.

When hunger becomes a barrier to learning, it can become a barrier to opportunity.

We believe every child deserves to begin the school day with the nourishment and support they need to learn, grow and thrive.

Through the Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative, we work to expand access to nutritious breakfast support for children who need it most — and to build partnerships capable of sustainable, scalable solutions.

From morning to classroom — a journey worth supporting.

Step 01 — Breakfast service

Prepared with care, served with respect.

A warm, nutritious meal can become the first act of belonging in a child’s school day. The service is not only food — it is a message that they are seen.

Eating together

Shared meals build friendships. They restore energy, belief and the simple comfort of being cared for — turning a routine into a community moment.

Step 03 — Ready to learn

Nourishment changes how the school day can begin.

With nourishment comes readiness — to listen, to participate, to imagine a future not limited by hunger. The morning sets the tone for everything that follows.

The programme

The Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative

Our programme is designed to help children who may otherwise begin their school day without a nutritious meal.

We work towards building partnerships with schools, communities, businesses, foundations, philanthropists and other organisations to support breakfast provision and develop sustainable models for expansion.

Our approach

  1. IdentifyWork with communities and relevant partners to identify need.
  2. MobiliseBring together funding, resources, expertise and community support.
  3. DeliverSupport nutritious breakfast provision for participating children.
  4. MeasureTrack reach, resources and outcomes.
  5. GrowUse learning and partnerships to expand the programme sustainably.

Our Impact

We are committed to understanding and reporting the difference our work makes. As the programme grows, we will track and report on:
