Why breakfast matters

More than breakfast.
A better start.

A hungry child can struggle to focus and take part in class. Wendoo works with schools and communities to help children begin the day nourished and ready to learn.

Breakfast is the first step: it supports concentration, participation and the confidence to keep learning.

The Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative is designed to help children who may otherwise begin their school day without a nutritious meal.

Our impact

Behind every
number is a child.

Wendoo’s School Breakfast Programme launched in February 2023 and has been delivered to schoolchildren in Nigeria.

Wendoo remains active. Funding constraints have reduced the scale and frequency of activities. Wendoo is committed to clear documentation and reporting so supporters can see what resources achieve.

Statistics show the impact. Stories show what that impact means.

Wendoo is seeking sustainable support to strengthen and restore its programme activities.

Why Wendoo?

Practical work.
Shared purpose.

Practical — We address hunger as a barrier to learning.

Community-focused — We work with schools and communities to build sustainable programmes.

Measurable — We track delivery and seek to demonstrate meaningful outcomes.

Partnership-led — We work with people who want to create lasting change.

Responsible growth — We aim to extend what works to more schools and communities.

Trust and accountability

Care for Children.
Care with Resources.
