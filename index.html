Why Wendoo exists

A child’s school day should not begin with hunger.

For many children, the school day begins without a nutritious meal.

Hunger can make it harder to concentrate, participate and make the most of the opportunity that education provides.

That’s where Wendoo comes in.
We believe breakfast is more than a meal. It is a practical first step towards giving children a better start to their school day.

Our track record

We started with one school.
Now we want to reach more.

Wendoo launched its School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative in February 2023, beginning with a pilot at Nkeze Primary School in Idumuje-Ugboko, Delta State.

Our pilot gave Wendoo a practical starting point from which to understand the needs of children, schools and communities and to develop our approach.

What we’ve learned
Our experience at community level has shown us that sustainable impact requires more than providing a meal. It requires strong relationships with schools, community participation, reliable resources, responsible delivery and transparent reporting. Our next phase is about strengthening the model, building sustainable partnerships and reaching more children.

Where we are today

Honest about where we are.
Ambitious about where we’re going.

Wendoo remains committed to the School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative, but funding constraints have limited the scale and frequency of programme delivery.

Rather than hide this, we believe in being transparent about where we are and what is needed to move forward.

Our focus now is to:

  • Strengthen the programme model.
  • Build sustainable funding partnerships.
  • Improve documentation and measurement.
  • Increase programme delivery.
  • Reach more schools and children.

The pilot was the beginning. It is not the destination.
