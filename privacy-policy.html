About this policy

This policy covers the Wendoo website. It does not cover information handled separately as part of school programmes, partnerships or other activities outside this website.

Browsing the website

You can read the website without creating an account. The website does not currently provide online forms or process payments.

When you visit, your browser sends technical information needed to request and display pages, such as your IP address and the page requested, to the website’s hosting service.

Cookies and tracking

The website’s pages do not currently include advertising trackers or analytics scripts, or set cookies through page scripts.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Other services

If you follow a link to another website or use a separate service to contact or support Wendoo, review that service’s privacy information before sharing personal details.
