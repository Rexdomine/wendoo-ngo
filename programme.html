Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative

Breakfast is the beginning.

Wendoo brings schools, communities and partners together around a simple first step: a nutritious school breakfast.

See how it works ↓
Nourish✳Take part✳Learn

Why it matters

A meal opens up the morning.

When children start the day hungry, concentrating and participating can be harder. Breakfast helps create a better starting point for the school day.

How it works

Local knowledge.
Shared action.

Our approach connects the people who know each school with the support needed to put breakfast on the table.

  • ◉

    Identify

    Listen to schools and communities to understand where breakfast support is needed.

  • ✳

    Mobilise

    Bring partners, local knowledge and resources together.

  • ◒

    Deliver

    Work with schools and communities to provide nutritious breakfasts.

  • ✦

    Measure

    Document delivery and learn what the programme is achieving.

  • ↗

    Grow responsibly

    Improve the model and reach more schools as reliable support becomes available.

Where we began

One school. A foundation to build on.

Nkeze
Primary
School.

Our programme launched in February 2023 at Nkeze Primary School in Idumuje-Ugboko, Delta State.

That first experience continues to guide how we develop the programme with care.
