Our programme

A better school day starts with breakfast.

No child should have to learn on an empty stomach.

Wendoo works with schools and communities in Nigeria to help children begin the day nourished and ready to learn.

How the programme works ↓

Nourish. Enable. Empower.

Breakfast is
the first step.

A hungry child can struggle to focus and take part in class.

Our school breakfast programme is designed to help children who may otherwise start the day without a nutritious meal. We work with schools, communities and partners towards sustainable, locally led breakfast provision.

How the programme works

Local need.
Shared responsibility.

From identifying schools to learning from delivery, our model brings local knowledge and practical support together.

  1. 01

    Identify

    Identify schools and communities where children may begin the school day without a nutritious breakfast.

  2. 02

    Mobilise

    Bring local partners and resources together to support school breakfast provision.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Work with schools and communities to provide nutritious breakfasts for children.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Track delivery and seek to understand outcomes, with a commitment to clear documentation and reporting.

  5. 05

    Grow responsibly

    Learn from delivery, improve the model and work towards reaching more schools as sustainable support becomes available.

The programme today

Building towards
lasting support.

Wendoo’s School Breakfast Programme launched in February 2023 and has been delivered to schoolchildren in Nigeria.

Wendoo remains active. Funding constraints have reduced the scale and frequency of activities. Sustainable support is needed to strengthen and restore programme activities.
