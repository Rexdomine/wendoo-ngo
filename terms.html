1. About these terms

These terms cover use of the Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative website. The website shares information about our work and ways to support it.

2. Using the website responsibly

Please use this website lawfully and respectfully. Do not try to disrupt the website, introduce harmful software, gain unauthorised access or misuse information about other people.

3. Our content

Please respect the rights attached to text, photographs, logos and other material on this website. Sharing a link to a page does not give permission to reuse its images or branding. Do not imply that Wendoo endorses you or your organisation without our agreement.

4. Information on this website

We aim to keep information clear and accurate. Programme activities and opportunities may change, and a page may not reflect the latest position. Please check with Wendoo before relying on information to make arrangements.

5. Supporting Wendoo

Information about donating, volunteering or partnering describes ways to support our work. It does not by itself confirm a placement, partnership or particular use of a contribution. Please review the details of the relevant opportunity before making a commitment.

6. Other websites

If you follow a link to another website, review its terms and privacy information before using its services or sharing personal information. Wendoo does not control the content or operation of other websites.

7. Changes to the website and these terms

We may update the website and these terms as our work develops. Please return to this page to read the current wording.

8. Questions

If you have a question about these terms or notice an error on the website, contact Wendoo.
