About Wendoo

A better start.
A shared purpose.

No child should have to learn on an empty stomach.

Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative is a Nigerian social-impact organisation with a clear purpose: helping children start the school day nourished, ready to learn and able to participate.

Founded around the belief that breakfast can be a foundation for a better school day, Wendoo works with schools, communities, partners and supporters to provide practical school breakfast programmes for children who need them most.

Founded with breakfast in mind. ↓

Founded with
breakfast in mind.

Wendoo was founded by Mrs Maureen Bond with a simple but important belief: when a child starts the school day hungry, their ability to concentrate, participate and make the most of their education can be affected.

Our journey began with a pilot school in Idumuje-Ugboko, Delta State, giving us a practical starting point from which to learn, develop our approach and understand what is required to deliver meaningful impact at community level.

From a proven starting point
to a bigger ambition.

Wendoo is building on this foundation with a clear programme model, accountable leadership and a commitment to responsible delivery and measurement.

Our ambition is to take school breakfast programmes to more children and more communities across Nigeria — while building the partnerships, resources and systems needed to make that growth sustainable.
