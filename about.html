About Wendoo

A better start.
A shared purpose.

No child should have to learn on an empty stomach.

We are Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative, a Nigerian NGO with a simple purpose: helping children start the school day nourished and ready to learn.

Get to know us ↓

01 / Our story

Founded with
breakfast in mind.

Wendoo was founded by Mrs Maureen Bond.

Our story starts with a clear purpose: school breakfast as a foundation for a better school day. That purpose is reflected in our name — Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative.

Wendoo was registered in Nigeria on 31 October 2022, under CAC No. 191090.

02 / Our mission

Nourished children.
Ready to learn.

To help children begin the school day with breakfast, so they are ready to learn and take part.
