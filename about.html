About Wendoo

A better start is built together.

Wendoo works with schools, communities and partners so more children can begin the school day nourished and ready to learn.

Meet our trustees

Our story

It began with a simple belief.

No child should have to learn on an empty stomach.

Founded by Mrs Maureen Bond, Wendoo began its school breakfast work at Nkeze Primary School in Idumuje-Ugboko, Delta State. That first school gave us a place to listen, learn and build a stronger approach.

How we work

Rooted in community.

A lasting breakfast programme takes more than meals. We work with schools and local people, build practical partnerships, and learn from each step before growing.

Our trustees

The people guiding Wendoo.

Our trustees bring experience in leadership, communication and community service to Wendoo's mission.

Founder & Chair of Trustees

Mrs Maureen Bond

A UK-based entrepreneur and philanthropist who founded Wendoo to address child hunger. She guides its vision and partnerships.

Trustee Secretary

Peter Agidee

With more than 25 years in marketing and strategic communication, Peter supports trustee coordination, records and stakeholder engagement.

Trustee

Louis Echefulam Anugwa

A retired Assistant Comptroller-General of the Nigeria Immigration Service, Louis brings 35 years of public service to governance and resource oversight.

Trustee

Pastor Alfred Osagie

A resident pastor and leadership coach who supports community outreach, family wellbeing and women's empowerment.
