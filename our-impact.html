A programme in practice

Wendoo’s School Breakfast Programme launched in February 2023 and began delivering breakfast support to schoolchildren in Nigeria.

Rooted in a community

Nkeze Primary School in Idumuje Ugboko, Delta State, is Wendoo’s first school and a focus for its programme development and delivery.

Active, with more to do

Wendoo remains active. Funding constraints have reduced the scale and frequency of activities. Sustainable support can help strengthen and restore the programme.

Our programme experience

Learning from
Nkeze Primary School.

Wendoo’s work in Idumuje Ugboko puts its school breakfast mission into practice at community level.

Nkeze Primary School was selected because of the challenges facing the school and its pupils, including a lack of running water and buildings in need of repair.

This experience gives Wendoo a foundation to learn from delivery and develop an approach that can be adapted for other schools and communities. Strengthening that foundation is the next step.
