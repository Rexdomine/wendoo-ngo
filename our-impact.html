Our 2023 pilot

One school.
Many better mornings.

At Nkeze Primary School in Idumuje-Ugboko, Delta State, Wendoo provided breakfast twice a week across two school terms.

The story behind the figures

Start local. Learn deeply.

Nkeze Primary School gave Wendoo a place to understand what breakfast delivery takes at community level.

The experience is shaping a stronger programme model, with schools, local relationships and responsible growth at its centre.
