Give a child a better start

A school day starts
with breakfast.

A meal today. A better opportunity tomorrow.

Every child deserves to arrive at school nourished, ready to learn and able to participate fully in the school day. Through the Wendoo School Breakfast Empowerment Initiative, we work with schools and communities in Nigeria to address hunger as a barrier to learning. Your generosity can help provide nutritious breakfasts, strengthen school participation and give children a better start.
