way to support breakfast.

Wendoo works with schools and communities in Nigeria to address hunger as a barrier to learning.

01

Offer your time

Interested in helping with Wendoo’s work? Think about the time you could offer and the kind of support you can provide.

Any role would need to be agreed with Wendoo, based on current needs and capacity.

02

Share your skills

Professional expertise can support the work behind each breakfast. Communications, technology and organisational support are ways to begin a conversation.

Consider a specific skill and the time you could commit.

03

Make a connection

Help others discover Wendoo. Share our website or introduce the initiative to people and organisations interested in school breakfast support.
